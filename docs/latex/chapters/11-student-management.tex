\chapter{Student Management}
\section{Status}
\implemented{} with mock-backed services.

\section{Routes}
Implemented student routes are \code{/students}, \code{/students/new}, \code{/students/[studentId]}, \code{/students/[studentId]/edit}, and \code{/students/[studentId]/placement}.

\section{Feature Files}
Student files exist under \code{src/features/students}. They include domain types, Zod schemas, mock data, lifecycle/document services, hooks, create/edit/lifecycle/document actions, directory/profile/forms/status badge/document panel components.

\section{Capabilities}
Student Management supports directory, create, edit, profile, lifecycle controls, documents, status badges, tenant-scoped access, and academic placement integration.

\section{Permissions}
Reads use \code{student.view}; mutations use \code{student.create}, \code{student.update}, \code{student.archive}, or \code{student.manage} depending on operation.
