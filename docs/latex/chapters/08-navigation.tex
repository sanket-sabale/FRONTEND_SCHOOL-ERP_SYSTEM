\chapter{Navigation Architecture}
\input{diagrams/navigation}
\section{Sidebar}
Navigation is configured in \code{src/config/navigation.ts}. Items are grouped into Overview, People, Admissions, Academics, Attendance, Finance, Communication, Insights, and Administration.

\section{Permission Filtering}
Sidebar and command palette results are filtered with \code{hasPermission}. Navigation items include required permissions and \code{activeMatch} patterns for active route behavior.

\section{Placeholders}
Several navigation items point to hash routes such as \code{/\#payments}, \code{/\#payroll}, \code{/\#admin}, \code{/\#reports}, \code{/\#academics}, and \code{/\#timetable}. These are placeholders or dashboard anchors, not full route implementations.

\section{Command Palette}
The command palette includes quick actions for students, guardians, staff, staff departments/designations, staff attendance/leave/documents/performance/academic assignments/workload, HR operations, attendance, academics, promotions, and reports.
