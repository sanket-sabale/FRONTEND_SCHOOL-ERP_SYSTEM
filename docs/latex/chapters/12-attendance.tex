\chapter{Attendance}
\section{Student Attendance}
Student attendance routes include \code{/attendance}, \code{/attendance/mark}, \code{/attendance/history}, \code{/attendance/history/[attendanceId]}, \code{/attendance/reports}, and \code{/attendance/export}.

\section{Integrity and Repair}
Attendance integrity routes include \code{/attendance/integrity}, detail, audit, repair queue, repair detail, and bulk repair routes.

\section{Mark Attendance UI}
The \code{/attendance/mark} route uses URL-backed date/class/section selection, tenant-scoped roster loading, status counters, per-student status/check-in/remarks fields, bulk mark-all-present, reset, and server action save.

\section{Staff Attendance}
Staff attendance routes are \code{/staff/attendance} and \code{/staff/attendance/history}. Staff attendance is implemented under \code{src/features/staff-attendance}.

\section{Permissions}
Attendance permissions include \code{attendance.view}, \code{attendance.mark}, \code{attendance.update}, \code{attendance.correct}, \code{attendance.history.view}, \code{attendance.manage}, \code{attendance.report}, and \code{attendance.integrity.view}.
