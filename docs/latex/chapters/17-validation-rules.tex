\chapter{Validation and Business Rules}
\section{Validation Architecture}
The app uses Zod schemas and service-level rules. Schemas live under feature \code{schemas} directories; domain rules live under feature \code{services/*-rules.ts}.

\section{Examples}
\begin{itemize}
\item Staff lifecycle transitions are centralized in \code{staff-rules.ts}.
\item Attendance date/status validation is centralized in attendance schemas/rules/services.
\item Promotion safeguards are implemented in promotion rules and batch/audit rules.
\item Staff academic assignment duplicate and primary-responsibility conflict checks are service-side.
\item Workload status and readiness thresholds are centralized in \code{staff-workload-rules.ts}.
\item Staff account linking rejects duplicate and cross-tenant scenarios through service rules.
\end{itemize}
