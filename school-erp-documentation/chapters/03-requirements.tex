\chapter{Requirements}
\section{Functional Requirements}
\begin{itemize}
\item Manage students, guardians, staff, academic structure, attendance, communication, promotions, and staff workload.
\item Enforce route-level and action-level permissions.
\item Preserve tenant, school, campus, and academic year scope for domain operations.
\item Provide responsive ERP interfaces for desktop, tablet, and mobile.
\end{itemize}

\section{Non-Functional Requirements}
\begin{itemize}
\item Maintainable modular monolith architecture.
\item TypeScript-first domain modeling.
\item Server-side validation through schemas and service rules.
\item Professional UI with light/dark theme tokens.
\item Build validation through TypeScript, ESLint, and Next.js production build.
\end{itemize}

\section{Security and Tenant Requirements}
\begin{securitybox}
Client-provided \texttt{tenantId} or \texttt{tenant\_id} must never be trusted as authorization authority. Tenant scope must come from trusted request/session context.
\end{securitybox}

\section{MVP and Future Distinction}
Implemented modules are documented as current. Future items such as payroll, recruitment, biometric integrations, WhatsApp/SMS, full timetable generation, and production database migrations are future or not implemented.
