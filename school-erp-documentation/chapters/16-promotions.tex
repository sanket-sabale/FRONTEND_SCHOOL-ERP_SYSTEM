\chapter{Promotions}
\section{Status}
\statusimplemented

\section{Promotion Candidate Lifecycle}
Student promotion functionality exists under \texttt{src/features/student-promotions}. Routes include promotion management, detail, bulk preview, and audit.

\section{Safety Boundaries}
The project distinguishes candidate lifecycle, controlled bulk preparation, and audit/evidence. Bulk preparation does not equal bulk application. Export preview in Stage 12 explicitly does not generate CSV, Excel, PDF, download URLs, or backend export jobs.

\section{Tenant Scope}
Promotion operations are academic-year and tenant scoped. Future production persistence should preserve evidence and audit trails.
